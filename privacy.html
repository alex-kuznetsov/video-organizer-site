Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

video-organizer is a personal tool operated by Alexander Kuznetsov for his own use only. The only Google account it accesses is his own.

Google data the app accesses

  • Google Drive (drive.file): creates folders and uploads files. It can see only the files it created itself.
  • YouTube (youtube): uploads videos as private, creates private playlists and adds videos to them, and reads the status of videos it uploaded.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to archive the owner's recordings in the owner's Drive and YouTube account. OAuth tokens and temporary copies of recordings are stored only on the owner's computer.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
